Why grey?

Black-bordered cards disappear against black. Mid-grey sits between a black border and white edge wear, so both stand out. Use this guide for black-bordered cards and the black guide for everything else. Keep the grey sides clean: dust or scuffs can look like defects.{% else %}

Why black?

The scanner looks up through the glass. With black behind the card and around it, the card's edges stand out sharply, so whitening, nicks and soft corners show up. Keep both black sides clean: dust or scuffs on the black can look like defects. For black-bordered cards, print the grey version of this guide.{% endif %}
